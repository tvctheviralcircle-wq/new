## Things the reference gets wrong (don't copy these)

- Caption typos and transcription errors left in ("sketch" for "get", "Diamond" for "didn't", "waster Time", "build in order"). Spelling must come from the script.
- Lower-case "i" in captions.
- Red keywords over a black shirt are hard to read in several shots. Use the lighter rose on dark clothing.
- A few giant behind-the-head words are unreadable because the head hides the middle letters.
- One callout ends with a stray question mark ("Leads don't pick up the call?").
