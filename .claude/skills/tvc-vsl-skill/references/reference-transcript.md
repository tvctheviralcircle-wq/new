## Structure worth reusing

1. Hook that names the viewer's current state and promises an uncomfortable truth (8 s).
2. The broken model, as a short list the viewer recognises (20 s).
3. The excuse loop: what "they" say, quoted (18 s).
4. Pivot: "I could show you proof. I won't. Instead, here's what's broken." (15 s)
5. The real problem, told from the customer's side (30 s).
6. Reframe in one line: you didn't need more X, you needed Y (6 s).
7. Who I am, in three clauses (15 s).
8. The system and its proof numbers (12 s).
9. What it does, layer by layer (30 s).
10. The guarantee, stated twice (25 s).
11. Call to action, then scarcity (25 s).
